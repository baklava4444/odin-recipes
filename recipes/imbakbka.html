Description

Libyan imbakbka (or mokhbka) is a popular, one-pot comfort food featuring pasta cooked directly in a rich, spiced tomato and meat broth. It is famous for its thick, saucy consistency and deep flavors infused with garlic, onions, chili, and warming spices.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Meat
  • Spices
  • Tomatoes paste
  • Aromatics
  • Chickpeas
  • Olive Oil

How to make it

  1. Sauté onions and garlic..
  2. Brown meat in pot.
  3. Add spices and tomato paste.
  4. Pour in chopped tomatoes.
  5. Add water and chickpeas.
  6. Simmer until meat is tender.
  7. Stir in dry pasta..
  8. Cook until pasta is soft.
  9. Serve hot in bowls
